An add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3539609975]Better Trees[/url]. With its "Render trees over colonists" option on, a dense forest hides everyone in it; this mod makes the canopy translucent wherever something stands under it, so you can still see who is where and what they are doing.

[h2]What it does[/h2][list]
[*]Pawns, the weapons in their hands, whatever they carry, items and corpses under a canopy show through it (40% by default), the same under one tree or many.
[*]Everything shows as it really is: fights, Melee Animation duels, crawling, aiming, hauling.
[*]Trees with nothing under them look exactly as before, and the canopy keeps swaying with them in the wind.
[*]Two trees of the same size side by side in one row no longer flicker in thin stripes where they overlap (see the gallery).
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2][list]
[*]See-through: 0% keeps canopies solid; 100% turns the mod's layer off completely.
[*]Layer resolution and refresh (default full resolution; the layer is only redrawn when the trees sway, change or the view moves). Lower them on a weaker graphics card.
[*]Hide Better Trees' cursor-transparency button (off by default). Its hotkey keeps working.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Works with any mod that draws trees over pawns: it moves those trees back below pawns and lays a translucent copy of the canopy on top. With vanilla tree layering it does nothing. Better Trees is not required.
[*]Bullets and effects over trees with nothing under them show as in vanilla, on top of the leaves.
[*]Cost: well under 1 ms per frame in our tests, even in a 73-pawn battle inside a dense forest. See the chart in the gallery.
[*]Tested on RimWorld 1.7.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[/list]
