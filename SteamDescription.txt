An add-on for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3539609975]Better Trees[/url]. With its "Render trees over colonists" option on, a dense forest hides everyone in it; this mod shows pawns and items under a canopy as a translucent ghost through the leaves, so you can still see who is where.

[h2]What it does[/h2][list]
[*]Pawns under a canopy show through it (40% by default). Parts that are not covered look the same as before.
[*]Items and corpses lying under trees show through too.
[*]The trees themselves are left alone, so nothing fades in and out as pawns walk around.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2][list]
[*]Ghost opacity; 0% turns it off.
[*]Animals and mechanoids on or off.
[*]Items and corpses on or off.
[*]Hide Better Trees' cursor-transparency button (off by default). Its hotkey keeps working.
[/list]

[h2]Notes[/h2][list]
[*]Works with any mod that draws trees over pawns; with vanilla tree layering it does nothing. Better Trees is not required.
[*]No ghost for: pawns in an animation (for example Melee Animation duels), crawling, carrying or being carried; packed furniture; things on shelves or other buildings; modded items with their own drawing.
[*]In a 73-pawn battle inside a dense forest the ghosts took about 1 ms per frame zoomed in and less zoomed out; see the chart in the gallery.
[*]Tested on RimWorld 1.7.
[*]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.
[/list]
